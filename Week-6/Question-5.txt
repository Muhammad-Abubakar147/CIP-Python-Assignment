Give It a Personality
A system message is a hidden first message that tells the AI who to be. The user never sees it. Use one to give your bot a personality:



Make a constant PERSONALITY with your bot's instructions: who it is, how it talks, and what it knows. Tell it to keep replies short.

In main, start messages with a system message instead of an empty list: messages = [make_message("system", PERSONALITY)].

Update format_messages so a system message becomes Instructions:  plus its content, followed by a blank line (two \n).



For example, for a pirate bot, format_messages returns:

Instructions: You are a pirate.

User: Hi!
Assistant:



Here is the personality we used. Invent your own!

PERSONALITY = "You are a super sassy queen. Keep replies short."
