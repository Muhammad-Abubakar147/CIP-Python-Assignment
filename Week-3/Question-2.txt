Great work! Let's add a few finishing touches to make this a fun game to play :)



Do the following:

Use get_random_word() so the secret word is different every game.

Accept any capitalisation by calling .lower() on the guess.

If the guess is not exactly 5 letters, print Please enter a 5-letter word. and ask again. A typo must not use up one of the six guesses. You will need a while loop inside the for loop to do this!



Example output:

Welcome to Wordle!
Enter your guess: cat
Please enter a 5-letter word.
Enter your guess: PLANT
Result: [p] [l] [a] n t
