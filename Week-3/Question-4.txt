A twist on Wordle from your section. Try it yourself, then compare your code with the solution.



Six guesses not enough? Or too many? At the start, ask the player how many guesses they want.



Here is a sample run:



Welcome to Wordle!
How many guesses do you want? 3
Enter your guess: crazy
Result: [c] r (a) z [y]
Enter your guess: corny
Result: [c] o r (n) [y]
Enter your guess: dance
Result: (d) [a] [n] (c) e
Better luck next time! The correct word was 'candy'.
